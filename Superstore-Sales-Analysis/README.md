# Superstore Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Superstore sales data to identify sales trends, top-performing categories, regions, customer segments, products, and states.

The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can support better sales and marketing decisions.

## 🎯 Business Questions

- What are the total and average sales?
- Which category generates the highest sales?
- Which region performs best?
- Which customer segment generates the most sales?
- Which sub-category performs best?
- Which products and customers generate the highest sales?
- How do sales change over time?
- Which months have the highest and lowest sales?
- How has sales performance changed year over year?

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- SQL
- Power BI
- Excel

## 📈 Key Findings

- Technology is the highest-performing category.
- West is the strongest-performing region.
- Consumer is the largest customer segment by sales.
- Phones is the leading sub-category.
- California generates the highest state-level sales.
- 2018 is the strongest year by sales.
- November records the highest monthly sales.
- February records comparatively low sales.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase marketing focus on high-performing Technology products.
- Maintain strong inventory levels for high-performing categories.
- Continue targeting the Consumer segment.
- Strengthen sales strategies in the West region.
- Prepare additional inventory before the November–December high-sales period.
- Consider promotional campaigns during lower-sales periods.
